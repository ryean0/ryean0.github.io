Anubys

Anubys is my portfolio product aimed at delivering scalable, cost-efficient & effective AI model infrastructure to guarantee accountable, reproducible, and retraceable ML workflows for enterprise-scale tooling and/or individual hobbyists.

Open anubys.dev All work

What it does

A merge check, with a verdict.

Anubys compares a candidate LLM agent to a baseline on paired scenarios in CI and returns a stats-backed merge check. It runs when a pull request opens. Production traffic does not pass through it.

  • Pass

    The gap stays inside the bar the suite set.

  • Block

    The evidence says the candidate is behind the baseline.

  • Insufficient evidence

    The interval is too wide to decide the merge.
